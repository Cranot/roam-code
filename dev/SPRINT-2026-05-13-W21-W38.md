# Sprint 2026-05-13 -- Waves 21 through 38

## Executive summary

Eighteen waves (W21 through W38) shipped uncommitted on top of the
2026-05-12 sign-off. The sprint had three orientations: (a) **CI
hardening** -- a global ``--ci`` lever plus opt-in gates that flip the
``pr-bundle`` exit code on ghost symbols; (b) **drift-guard
infrastructure** -- roughly 14 new structural defences that lint, gate,
or pin counts/anchors/citations to AST-derived ground truth so that
documentation and source cannot diverge silently; and (c) **plugin
substrate dogfood** -- a typed ``RoamPluginContext`` shipped in W22.5 and
then validated end-to-end by extracting Rails detection out of core
into ``dev/example-plugin/roam_plugin_rails`` (W28.2). Four real
dogfood-corpus fixes landed alongside (auth-gaps depth bump, slugger
underscore + whitespace, algo dependent-write predicate, Laravel dead
top-7 idioms). The sprint **does not commit**; the working tree carries
324 modified files + 167 untracked entries (+14,980 / -1,939 LOC) and
is ready for review.

## Recurring patterns surfaced

1. **Closed-enumeration over free-string composition** (CLAUDE.md
   Constraint 8). Five waves produced enumerations protected by AST
   lints: ``_SARIF_CONSUMERS`` (W22.3 + W26.1), ``_MODE_EXTRAS``
   phantom-verb check (W37.1), ``_DEPRECATED_COMMANDS`` (W21), the
   MCP-wrapper skip taxonomy (W22.4), and the constant-citation
   suffix scan (W36.6 / W37.2).

2. **Drift caught at AST ground truth, not regex**. W31.1 (regex)
   reported the wrong LAW 4 anchor count because of an embedded ``)``
   in a comment; W33.1 (AST) confirmed CLAUDE.md was already correct.
   Same lesson applies to W37.2's citation lint and W23.1's count-drift
   gates -- regex-only drift checks ship false alarms.

3. **Single source of truth via materialisation, not duplication**.
   W37.1 (modes), W37.5 (DOC_EXTENSIONS), W23.1 (count-drift scripts)
   all replaced hand-maintained twins with one canonical site and a
   derivation rule. Tests pin the derivation, not the duplicate.

4. **CI gating as composition, not new flags**. W21.6's ``--ci`` does
   not introduce new behaviour; it flips the defaults of existing
   per-command flags (``--leaks-only``, ``--strict``,
   ``--strict-resolved``). Explicit user intent always wins (LAW 11).

5. **Plugin substrate validated by extraction, not stubs**. W22.5
   shipped the contract; W25.1 (Path B shadow) and W28.2 (Path A
   clean-cut) proved it by removing Rails from core. Stub-only
   substrates ship with hidden coupling.

6. **Advisory-first, then ratchet**. W22.4 (MCP wrapper coverage),
   W25.2 (--budget coverage), W26.4 (mode classification),
   W37.2 (citation lint) all ship as xfail/threshold tests that
   surface drift without blocking unrelated PRs. Each carries a
   ``_THRESHOLD`` or ``_NO_CITATION_NEEDED`` allowlist that ratchets
   DOWN as gaps close.
