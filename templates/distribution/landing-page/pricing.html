Proposed definitions and billing

  • Active PR author: a unique human author of a reviewed pull request in the trailing 30 days. Bots and generated release-PR authors would not count.
  • Review: one posted structural-risk result. Re-runs on the same PR within 24 hours would count as one review.
  • Repository: one Git remote. A monorepo would count as one unless split billing is agreed.
  • Usage limits: the proposal is to notify the organisation admin at 80% and show a dashboard notice at 100%. Further reviews would pause at the cap unless the customer upgrades or opts into overage. No automatic overage charges.
  • Renewals: the proposal retains the package through the next renewal, with increases capped at 15% unless a cap was exceeded for two consecutive months.
  • Trial and open source: a 14-day trial and free Review for public open-source repositories are planned. The local CLI and MCP server are already free.
  • Billing: monthly or annual for Starter, Team, and Business; annual for Scale. The agreed rate would be fixed for the committed term.

Proposed rates were published in June 2026. Read the refund policy and confirm written terms before any payment.

Before you choose

Is anything held back from the free tools?

The CLI and MCP server are free under Apache 2.0 for individuals and teams. A paid report buys interpretation and a defined deliverable, not access to otherwise locked local checks. Your agent's model charges are separate.

What if we already have an AI code reviewer?

Keep the tools that work for you. Roam adds local codebase connections and structural checks for your agent or CI to use. Try it on a real task before deciding whether it adds useful information to your existing process.

Can we try the report without paying?

Yes. Run the local sample or read the linked self-audit example. Paid reports add review of the agreed scope and a founder walk-through. A sample does not establish that the findings will be useful on every repository.

Can we buy Review or Cloud today?

No. Both hosted products are planned and not available to subscribe to. An email enquiry records interest, not a purchase, reserved place, or launch commitment. You can use the free local tools now.

What should we send in a first email?

Describe the question you want answered, your languages, and the rough scope. A public repository link is optional. Do not email source code, credentials, or private reports. Agree an appropriate sharing path first.
