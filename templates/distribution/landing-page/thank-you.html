This page explains what happens after an engagement is agreed. It does not confirm a payment, booking, or repository access. Your written confirmation and agreed terms are the source of truth.

What happens next

Once your engagement is confirmed, the agreed scope and timeline guide these three steps. If you are unsure about your status, email hello@roam-code.com.

  1. 1 Agree the scope and schedule. You'll receive an email from hello@roam-code.com with the one-page SOW for countersignature, a read-only deploy-key invitation to the temporary working tree we'll use, and a link to schedule the walk-through call.
  2. 2 Start on the agreed kickoff date. Once the SOW is countersigned and the deploy key is approved, the replay can begin on that date. Team report ships within 5 business days. Deep report ships within 10 business days from kickoff.
  3. 3 Walk-through call + report delivery. Founder walks the report with your team for 30 minutes (Team) or 90 minutes (Deep), answers questions, and discusses the recommended CI gates. The polished Markdown + PDF lands right after the call.

After delivery: temporary clone is deleted, the engagement record is yours on request. Any optional 50% credit is toward a future Roam Review subscription, not cash or a refund. Read the future-credit conditions and your agreed terms.

Try the free local sample Run roam pr-replay --tier sample in a checkout with the required history. It checks the HEAD~5..HEAD commit range by default, not five PR identities, and does not rerun tests. Use it to explore the report format and bring questions to the walk-through. See the sample prerequisites.
Receipts Once self-serve checkout (Stripe) is live, you'll receive a Stripe payment receipt at the email used at checkout. For email-based orders we send the invoice + receipt with the SOW. EU-compliant tax invoice from our accountant attached alongside. If you need anything specific (PO number, billing address change), reply to the receipt.
Privacy + handling The temporary clone we use is deleted within 7 days of report delivery. We never train on, reuse, or retain buyer code. Full terms in the DPA + security policy.
Need to cancel? Pre-kickoff cancellations are full refund — see the refund policy. Reply to the receipt or email hello@roam-code.com with the order ID.
