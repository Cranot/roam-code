Maintenance windows

Site changes use the existing Cloudflare Pages deployment process; this page cannot confirm a rollout or uninterrupted availability. Future paid services (Roam Cloud, Roam Review) will document their maintenance windows in the per-service SLA, with at least 48-hour advance notice for non-emergency work.

Service-level commitments

Roam's marketing site (this domain) is best-effort, no SLA. Paid services target 99.5% monthly uptime after general availability, per the Terms of Service; while pre-launch / early-access they're best-effort. Private-deployment pilot SLAs are documented in individual order forms. See Trust for security posture and Governance for the control framework.
