Accessibility

Statement updated: 2026-09-13 — not a new accessibility audit

We build roam-code.com to be usable by everyone, including users with disabilities. Our target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance.

Approach and verification limits

Our May 2026 self-assessment covered an earlier seven-page site. It does not establish conformance of the redesigned pages or interactive code atlas. This statement describes our approach; it is not a conformance certification.

  • Contrast and focus — shared color tokens, visible focus styles and selected source-level contrast checks. These do not measure every rendered combination.
  • Skip-to-content link — present on every page; visible on focus
  • Semantic HTML — <nav>, <main>, <footer> landmarks used correctly; one <h1> per page
  • Reduced motion — @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) disables hover transforms
  • Map controls — the atlas has a labelled area chooser, keyboard handlers and a static fallback. Its informative image has descriptive alternative text; decorative marks are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Tables — comparison/pricing tables use captions and header scopes, inside named scrollable regions. Actual screen-reader behavior needs separate testing.

Known gaps

  • The visual map is not a complete text description of every connection. The area chooser and adjacent details provide another way to explore it.
  • Long code examples and wide tables can require scrolling. Their usability with enlarged text and assistive technology needs continuing review.

What our source checks cover

  • Selected color-pair calculations, shared typography and focus styles
  • Navigation, landmarks, labels and table markup
  • Map loading, retry and keyboard handlers in a controlled DOM test

Not yet tested with: real screen-reader sessions (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS) or assistive-tech users. We commit to a real screen-reader-driven audit before enabling any paid product.

EU Accessibility Act

The European Accessibility Act (EAA, Directive 2019/882) takes effect 28 June 2025 for many digital services. Marketing / information sites are largely exempt; e-commerce and SaaS delivery surfaces are not. We aim to meet the EAA scope before any paid service launches.

Report an issue

If anything on roam-code.com is hard or impossible to use with your assistive technology, email hello@roam-code.com with what you tried and what happened. We respond within 72 hours on business days and prioritise accessibility fixes ahead of feature work. See also: security policy.

The update date records a wording correction, not a fresh browser or screen-reader assessment.
