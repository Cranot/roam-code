# Review the product a reader will infer

Use for a material positioning revision or a behavioral skill trial, not every
typo. This is a diagnostic, not a numerical writing score or a slogan generator.
Product authority remains in the repository orientation and current owner brief.

## Cold reading before explanation

Give a fresh reader only the draft and its intended audience/surface, without
product docs, intended answers, past criticism or the author's rationale. For an
opening, show headline and supporting text first; then show the complete section.
Ask what product they believe this is, what it returns or does, who uses that
result, why they would add it to their existing tools, and what they expect next.
Ask them to point to the words that produced that reading. Accept "not stated."
Keep their inferred claims separate from claims the draft actually makes.

Then review against the relevant sources. Check product identity, useful
benefit-with-mechanism, actor, claim support, scope and the surface's job
separately. A persuasive but unsupported claim and a true but unhelpful sentence
are different failures. An explanation cannot be supplied only in review notes.

## Stress the meaning, not one favorite phrase

For an opening, separate the agent's job from the added tool. An introduction
that promises to help an agent understand, find problems and solve them may
describe real benefits but still omit Roam's contribution. Ask which visible
words identify a callable analysis/check, its result and the agent's use of it.
Do not fill in the missing mechanism from your source knowledge. A generic
benefit is not rescued by adding another generic benefit. Conversely, do not
reject useful plain category wording merely because another tool shares it.

When the owner requests self-evaluation, run this as an explicitly author-led
review. Preserve the current opening, a capable baseline and rejected candidates.
Test the original misunderstanding, a different surface and a valid narrow
passage that should be retained. Record reasons and source support; the author's
familiarity with the product prevents this from being a cold reading or a causal
measurement of the skill. A supported application decision need not claim either.

Choose cases that could change the writing decision:

- A broad entry point must not collapse to a code map, generic bug finder,
  automatic fixer, or review-only tool. It need not enumerate every command.
- An algorithm-focused passage must explain the candidate approach and the
  agent's applicability check. Merely saying "understand code" loses that value;
  claiming the faster replacement is already established overstates it.
- Healthy-code work can still be useful: a dependency answer, a comparison or a
  completed check. Do not manufacture a defect to make the product worth using.
- A paid-service passage must explain the actual deliverable and preserve its
  availability and terms. Free tooling and model-free static analysis do not
  make the connected model or every service free.
- A valid narrow reference entry or good existing paragraph should survive
  without an inserted product manifesto. Consistency is meaning, not repetition.

In skill development, preserve the incoming guidance and output. Compare a
candidate with capable writing from the same source packet without the skill;
if the authority also changes, do not credit all improvement to the skill.
Keep failed drafts, ties and regressions. After a useful correction, test a
different surface or objection rather than rehearsing only the original case.
No phrase blacklist or fixed feature count proves that readers get the product.

Use fixed supporting copy to isolate an H1 edit. If a candidate's value overlaps
that copy, also compare coherently paired complete openings before ruling it
out on redundancy. Keep the two interventions separate; retained owner
acceptance is a decision constraint, not evidence of superiority.

## Check the reading path

For comparisons, distinguish the tool's actor, delivery method and analysis
method before turning them into categories. Static, structural and algorithmic
analysis can overlap; CLI and MCP are both agent interfaces. A tidy matrix must
not imply exclusivity the implementation does not have. Compare the same kind
of question on both sides, retain real differences and dated source boundaries,
and let a useful narrow comparison survive without a complete market taxonomy.

Read the skim path separately: headings, buttons, captions and dominant examples.
What would those elements lead someone to expect before they read the full prose?
Then compare with the full reading. A diagram's arrows and outcome labels are
claims too: trace them to the implemented operations, and distinguish illustrative
results from observed output. A qualification far below cannot repair a prominent
promise of automatic work or guaranteed outcomes. Attach the relevant scope to
the element making that promise without turning every button or narrow entry
into a product definition. Record a concrete misunderstanding or a retain decision;
do not replace this check with a ban on diagrams, technical terms or numbers.

For a dense page or README, name the reader's next decision and sketch the
shortest supported route to it before rearranging sections. A new user should
reach a suitable setup and first useful result without traversing benchmark
history or an exhaustive reference. An existing user should still find the
specific reference they came for. This is not a fixed section order or word limit.

Test the proposed route from the reader's current step, following the actual
links and instructions. Match each promised result to the linked operation;
a nearby command for a different job does not fulfill the promise. Keep feature
availability visible with the feature, and put configuration and restart steps
in its specific guide. A qualification earns repetition when it protects a
distinct entry point or changes the reader's decision; a focused operating
guide may need the full detail. Preserve cost, prerequisites and incomplete
results rather than moving claim-changing limits out of sight.
Check public destinations against the publication candidate, not just a local
file: an ignored generated result can make a broken public link look valid.
Move unique evidence intact to its maintained home with its date, scope and
losses; document any objective correction instead of silently preserving a false
claim. Retain anchor compatibility and generated ownership. Do not replace
useful detail with a vague "learn more" link or create another catch-all guide.
A focused reference may need no reorganization at all.

## Disposition

Record revise, retain, or unresolved with the actual reading and source support.
Give a material out-of-scope finding a disposition in the existing work sequence:
a named next action, a reason to retain it, or a deferred trigger. Do not let
useful findings disappear into trial notes or create a second backlog.
Keep factual validity, product interpretation, reader preference, owner acceptance
and publication state separate. A model reading is not human comprehension or
conversion evidence. Unavailable independent review stays unavailable. Iterate
on an observed misunderstanding, not indefinitely toward a self-awarded "gold"
label, and do not apply test-first drafts without the requested acceptance.
Honor an explicit delegation of that decision to the author. After authorized
application, compare the selected text with the served page and its metadata;
an unapplied correction remains an open website task even if the skill improved.
