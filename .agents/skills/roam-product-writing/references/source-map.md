Read current sources, not a remembered pitch. Missing sources stay a named limit.
Do not duplicate the manuals into this skill.

- **Product:** `docs/understanding-roam.md`, especially **The product model behind
  the words** in **How we speak**, plus the mechanism and limits relevant to
  the passage. Historical quotations are not current copy instructions. The
  latest explicit owner correction outranks a remembered headline or prior

  sources when relevant, not as mandatory examples or proof of publication.

- **Numbers:** maintained generated blocks and qualified published measurements.

- **Metadata and promises:** `pyproject.toml`, `codemeta.json`, and the relevant
  registry card or generator. Check the actual destination of a named guide and
